Our legacy

A proud football tradition

Football at Isipathana has always been more than a game. Around the turn of the century, generations of Pathanians made our school one of the strongest football names in Sri Lanka, winning Colombo District titles from Under-14 to Under-18, National Championships at Under-16 and Under-18, the Under-17 title and the Brazil Cup at Under-20. Every squad since has played to honour that standard, and in 2026 the Pathanians returned to Division 1 and to the national jerseys of Sri Lanka.

  • U-14Colombo District Champions & National Runners-Up1999
  • U-17Champions1999
  • U-16Colombo District & National Champions2000 · 2001
  • U-18Colombo District & National Champions2002 · 2003
  • U-20Brazil Cup Champions2002

Academy & scholarships

Play for Isipathana

We are looking for skilled, hard-working footballers, especially from outside Colombo, who want to develop their game while continuing their education at Isipathana College.

Grade 9

Junior academy entry

  • For players seeking entry to Grade 9
  • Train with the college squads and coaches
  • Football scholarship support for outstanding players*
After O/L

Senior entry for A/L

  • For students who have completed the G.C.E. O/L
  • Join the senior squad while studying for the A/L
  • Football scholarship support for outstanding players*

What we look for

  • Strong football skills and game sense
  • Commitment to training and to studies
  • Good discipline and attitude
  • School, club or district football experience

Selected applicants may be invited to a football trial at the college ground. Parents or guardians are welcome to attend.

Important notice on admission

Isipathana College is a government school. All admissions, whether to Grade 9 or to the G.C.E. A/L after the O/L, are made only by the Principal of Isipathana College in accordance with the laws, regulations and circulars of the Ministry of Education of Sri Lanka in force at the time, including any approval required from the Ministry or the relevant education authority.

  • The Isipathana College Football Development Committee does not admit students and has no authority to grant, promise or guarantee admission to the school.
  • Submitting an application, attending a football trial, being selected for a squad, or being offered football scholarship support is not an offer or promise of admission. A player can only join the academy after being lawfully admitted to the school.
  • *Football scholarship support is provided solely from funds raised by the Football Development Committee, for football-related needs, and is subject to available funds, the player's conduct, attendance and studies, and the rules of the school. It may be reviewed or withdrawn, and creates no right to admission or to continued support.
  • No fee, donation or payment of any kind is required to apply, to attend a trial, or in connection with admission. No person is authorised to request or accept money for admission or selection on behalf of the school or the committee. Please report any such request to the Principal.
  • Information submitted in the application is used only to assess the player for the football programme and may be shared with the school administration.

Issued by the Isipathana College Football Development Committee. In the event of any conflict, the decision of the Principal of Isipathana College and the applicable Ministry of Education regulations prevail.
